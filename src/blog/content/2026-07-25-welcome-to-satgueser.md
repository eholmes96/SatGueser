Welcome all! I developed this game because I love geography and trivia, among other things. I've probably spent years browsing around Google Earth & Google Maps Satellite view just zooming into random places around the world. You can find some amazing stuff just browsing around! I highly recommend.

I love geography games like GeoGuessr, Globle, and Worldle so I have to give them some credit. I was also inspired by YouTube and Instagram accounts like @soluna16 and @bradyyourtutor. So check those out if you haven't yet.

I started off building the U.S. Cities version, then expanded to Global, and then eventually I added a daily challenge and then just recently, adding islands. Each one has their own perks and challenges, and I hope you enjoy all of them. I added a backend so you can login and track your stats, eventually I plan on having points leaderboards, but before that I will probably want to have enough users that I don't have the high score on every single one lol.

We're up to 55 cities on US, 62 on global, and 51 islands, and I try to add more locations every few days.

If you have any questions, you can email me at [feedback@satgueser.app](mailto:feedback@satgueser.app)
